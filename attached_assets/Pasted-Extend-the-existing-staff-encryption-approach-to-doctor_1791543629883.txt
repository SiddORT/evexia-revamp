- Name
- Phone and alternate phone numbers
- Email address
- Date of birth
- Full address: address lines, landmark, city, state, country and pincode
- Other personal contact or identifying information present in these tables

Requirements:

1. Inspect and reuse the existing encryption implementation in `staff_crypto.py`. Use authenticated encryption and secure key management.
2. Store encrypted values in appropriately named ciphertext columns and update SQLAlchemy models and Alembic migrations.
3. Use keyed blind indexes for fields that require exact-match searching or uniqueness checks. Do not use plain hashes for sensitive values.

7. Preserve existing patient, doctor and MR relationships, foreign keys, business identifiers and workflow behaviour.
8. Include tests for encryption/decryption, searching, migration safety, authorization and existing workflows.
9. Keep registration numbers, employee codes, patient codes and other operational identifiers usable where required. Assess each field individually rather than encrypting blindly.
10. Do not change unrelated fields or implement historical versioning in this task; historical data will be handled separately.
