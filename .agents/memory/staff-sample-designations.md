---
name: Staff sample designations
description: Why browser-local staff samples begin without designation assignments
---

Fictional staff samples should not cause a hidden initialization of Designation Master or claim to hold a real designation when none exists.

**Why:** Designation Master is independently managed and may be empty on first visit. Assigning made-up designation names to staff would imply those choices are valid when they are not; seeding another master as a side effect of opening Staff Management would alter unrelated browser data.

**How to apply:** Show sample entries as unassigned and guide admins to add active designations before assigning or creating staff. Preserve existing user-managed designation records and avoid manufacturing master entries just to satisfy the samples.
