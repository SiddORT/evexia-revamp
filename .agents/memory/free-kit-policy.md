---
name: Free KIT classification policy
description: Permanent free classification, independently selected count periods and no financial contribution.
---

Only protected Super Admin may classify an already saved Confirmed KIT order
as Free kit before rates have ever been saved. Zero-priced normal orders are
not free. Free status is irreversible, blocks ordinary corrections and pricing,
does not approve the order and never refunds quota after rejection/fulfillment.

Both independent global caps apply: Per MR and Per Doctor, each with its own
Financial year or Month selector. Count saved orders by saved identities/date,
not quantities, current directory ownership or marking date. April–March FYs
and calendar months use Asia/Kolkata. Unconfigured limits block marking; zero
allows none.

**Why:** These were explicit approved product boundaries, not implementation
defaults or inferred financial policy.

**How to apply:** Keep free orders operationally visible but outside all money,
pending-pricing, debt, completeness and MR achievement calculations. Preserve
the separately approved saved Business Parking basis for normal KIT achievement.
Do not expand this classification to drafts, MR self-service or other families.
