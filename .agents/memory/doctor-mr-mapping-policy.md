Doctor Area is a saved assignment from the existing Headquarters/Areas catalogue,
not a postal-address interpretation. Existing Doctors begin with Area unset.
Area corrections are independent of MR assignment and do not authorize coverage.

**Why:** The user explicitly confirmed this meaning of Area while requesting a
separate Admin Doctor–MR Mapping page.

**How to apply:** Never infer or backfill Doctor Area from addresses or a current
MR's geography. Current-MR HQ/Zone filters retain their own meanings.

Reuse the authoritative exact Doctor location Area introduced by the territory
work; do not create a second mapping-only Area. Retain existing explicit MR
eligibility rules without treating an Area selection as a coverage grant.

**Why:** The territory feature merged before the mapping feature and already
owned the saved Zone/HQ/Area tuple.

**How to apply:** Area corrections retain consistent parent geography and their
own concurrency version/reason; preserve legacy unset facts and never infer
territory or new ownership.

A Doctor has one current MR. Transfers retain the linked-Patient atomic move,
require a meaningful reason, and do not reassign saved orders or financial facts.
Preexisting mappings are current-state baselines with unknown original assignment
date/reason, not invented historical assignments.

**Why:** These are the user's explicit mapping and historical-evidence boundaries.

**How to apply:** Keep future assignment entry points on the same history-producing
transactional path; retain historical identity snapshots and original archive
decryption keys after later renames or deactivation.
