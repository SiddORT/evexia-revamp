description: Approved KIT contribution formulas and the dashboard-only unpaid-plus-unverified measure.
---

The four MR Overview cards are shared all-order cards, not KIT-only cards.
Only KIT contributions are defined so far: Total Sales sums saved Business
Parking; Gross and Actual Sales intentionally both sum saved invoice payable
totals. Other type contributions must remain explicitly unconfigured, not zero.
Sales use existing approved-state eligibility and selected saved-order-date
FY/quarter attribution by saved MR owner, including Doctor-less supply orders.

**Why:** The user approved these exact definitions and deferred formulas for
Immunotherapy, SPT and Lupin rather than authorizing inferred contributions.

**How to apply:** Disclose configured-subtotal scope. Keep Parking availability
independent of invoice availability and expose only minimal authorized amounts.
Do not reinterpret historical discount-derived contribution snapshots.

Dashboard Due Amount is ordinary unpaid plus committed unverified allocation
amounts, once each; equivalently payable minus verified receipts minus active
credits. ₹1,000 payable with ₹300 unverified means ₹700 unpaid plus ₹300
unverified = ₹1,000 Due; verification changes the card to ₹700. Fully received
but entirely unverified ₹1,000 remains ₹1,000 on this card.

**Why:** The user's latest clarification superseded subtracting all receipts for
this dashboard card only, not the existing ledger balance.

**How to apply:** Use allocation-level verification and existing obligation
lifecycle rules, including returned payable orders and complete receipt/credit
history. Keep ledger, Doctor balances, posting, enforcement and achievement
separate; never add whole order totals because some cash is unverified.
