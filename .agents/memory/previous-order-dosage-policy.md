---
name: Previous order dosage policy
description: Product approval for Immunotherapy order-entry suggestions without administration or clinical inference.
---

Immunotherapy next-dosage assistance is based on the latest authorized saved
order, including pending/rejected orders, not administered treatment. Optional
PD/DD choices are retained; the user's preliminary PD/PP example does not
authorize adding PP. No automatic choice follows B8.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested an editable order-entry convenience
while excluding clinical recommendations, mandatory escalation, broader
history permissions and treatment tracking.

**How to apply:** Keep previous-order suggestions separate from Patient Dosage
History and administration evidence. Do not restrict history to approved or
delivered orders or disclose another MR's history after patient reassignment.
