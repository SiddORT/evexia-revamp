Keep MR Doctor access self-only. MR-head mapping is explicitly deferred; Staff
reporting managers and legacy MR-manager links must not imply hierarchy
permissions or cross-MR Doctor access.

**Why:** The user explicitly deferred mapping while requesting responsive
navigation, scoped assignment filters, and masked Doctor contacts.

**How to apply:** Treat Zone/Headquarter controls as filters of the signed-in
MR's saved assignments, not as team or authorization expansion. Preserve Admin
contact access and original stored contacts when changing MR presentation.
