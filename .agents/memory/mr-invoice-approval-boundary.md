---
name: MR invoice approval boundary
description: Why the new MR invoice configuration must not retrofit older document types.
---

The reference PDF approves a distinct Settings-only **MR invoice** preview,
not replacing or adapting the existing **Immunotherapy order invoice** document.
Keep the approval boundaries separate even though their reference layouts share
the same section hierarchy.

**Why:** The user explicitly required existing document types to remain
unchanged and excluded live invoice issuance and the concurrent MR Accounts work.
Similarity of reference material is not permission to migrate older samples,
their defaults, legal text or financial behavior.

**How to apply:** A future request concerning one invoice type must identify
that type before changing shared renderers, saved defaults or business actions.
Do not silently expand the MR invoice configuration to older document types.
