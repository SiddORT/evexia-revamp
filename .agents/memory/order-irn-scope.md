description: User-approved sales IRN phase and explicit deferred invoice boundary.

IRNs cover Kits & Consumables, SPT, Lupin and Immunotherapy, but only as manually
saved external metadata on approved orders under existing approval permissions.
They are separate from server-owned invoice references.

**Why:** The user confirmed all four sales families and explicitly chose
“Save IRNs only for now.”

**How to apply:** Do not infer approval for live invoice generation, viewing,
printing, download, issuance, IRNs on issued invoices, provider verification,
correction/clearing, bulk transfer or wider staff/MR permissions from IRN saving.
