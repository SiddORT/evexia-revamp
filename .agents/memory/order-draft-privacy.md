---
name: Order draft privacy
description: Why shared order permission is not authority over another creator's incomplete draft.
---

Immunotherapy, Kits and Lupin incomplete drafts are creator-private even for
Super Admin. Having permission to add/review normal orders is necessary but is
not draft-sharing or reassignment authority. MR drafts additionally retain the
authenticated MR scope. SPT keeps its existing durable draft access contract.

**Why:** The user required isolated resumable incomplete entry, no draft sharing
or reassignment, no access to another MR, and no expansion of staff permissions.
Incomplete private working state is distinct from a submitted clinical order.

**How to apply:** Keep draft ownership independent of the currently selected MR
or Doctor and of normal submitted-order review privileges. Do not silently add
cross-creator draft lists as a consequence of broader order permissions.
