---
name: Outgoing email presentation policy
description: Branding changes do not authorize stored-template rewrites or new notification delivery.
---

Treat email modernization as presentation only. Preserve customized source,
subjects, security notices, authorized facts and destinations; do not activate
channels, add recipients or replay old/uncertain notifications.

**Why:** The user explicitly limited modernization to readability and hierarchy,
not business policy or delivery activation. Authored wording and saved facts
must not be overwritten to obtain consistent branding.

**How to apply:** Prefer runtime presentation over stored-template upgrades.
Recognize untouched defaults narrowly, retain existing template-version guards,
and keep complete authored branded layouts from gaining another branded card.
If a later change truly needs persisted source updates, obtain separate scope
and preserve honest versions/provenance instead of disguising it as styling.
