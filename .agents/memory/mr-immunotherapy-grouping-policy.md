---
name: MR Immunotherapy grouping policy
description: Scope and historical boundary of the user's category/Mix composition rule.
---

New MR Immunotherapy placement groups same-category Mix allergens into SUBs of
at most three; every No Mix allergen is alone. Stable category identity, not
category display name, determines eligibility. Incomplete drafts are not placed
orders and must not consume real SUB identities.

**Why:** The user explicitly narrowed this policy to new MR placement, including
final placement of resumable incomplete drafts. It supersedes the earlier
unrestricted composition policy only within that scope.

**How to apply:** Preserve saved category/Mix evidence and membership after
placement. Master changes and reads cannot regroup saved orders. Compatible
edits retain issued identities without automatic merging, repacking or
renumbering. Historical orders, their distinct occurrences, explicit conversion
and unrelated Admin-created compositions keep their existing policy.

Suborder IDs and visual suborder separation are Admin-only. MR Immunotherapy
entry, edit and saved details present allergens without suborder headings.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked not to show suborder ID separation to MR.

**How to apply:** Flatten presentation only. Keep server grouping, saved
membership, identifiers, pricing and validation unchanged.
