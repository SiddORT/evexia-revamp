---
name: Staff role unification
description: Approved staff permission-role convergence and legacy conflict handling
---

Staff form Role and workspace permission assignment are one authoritative saved
role reference. Selecting Data Manager must assign its actual permissions.
Workspace login enablement remains an independent explicit setting.

**Why:** The user explicitly superseded the earlier separation between business
role labels and permission assignments.

**How to apply:** Do not restore a metadata-only staff Role selector. Reconcile a
legacy label only when it uniquely matches a current role and does not conflict
with an existing assignment. Preserve conflicting permissions until an
administrator explicitly resolves the choice; never infer Employee Codes.
