---
name: MR sales achievement policy
description: User-approved achievement accounting basis and separation from collections.
---
Only approved orders count toward MR sales achievement. Use the saved order date
and saved MR ownership under the April–March financial year, not approval date,
placement alone, payment flags or settlements. Progressing an approved order to
Shipped or Delivered must not remove its contribution; returned orders do not
contribute.

**Why:** The user explicitly confirmed approved orders as the achievement basis.

**How to apply:** Preserve this rule when extending MR reporting. Do not turn
achievement into collections, delivered-only accounting or target administration.
