name: Kits Business Parking meaning
description: Independent invoice-neutral Parking with explicitly approved saved contribution formulas.
---

Business Parking on Kits & Consumables orders is independently entered and is
never an invoice charge/deduction or inferred from taxable/payable totals. The
user subsequently approved a separate contribution policy: for Parking P and
percentage d, sales = P − P×d/100 and achievement = P − P×2d/100; for fixed INR D,
sales = P − D and achievement = P − 2D. Both deductions use original P. Do not
convert fixed rupees to a percentage, compound reductions or cap negative results.

**Why:** The original screenshot was only a field/layout reference; these
formulas are a later explicit user instruction, not an inference from it.

**How to apply:** Preserve invoice-neutrality and missing-versus-zero meaning.
Save contribution facts only on financial changes; preserve historical facts.
Dashboard/report integration is deferred and requires separate approval. Stored
facts alone do not establish earned credit: approval, saved owner/date and
exclusion of returned/rejected orders remain separate requirements.
