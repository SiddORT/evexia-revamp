name: Weekly low stock policy
description: Approved independent zero-stock alert and weekly recipient delivery boundaries.
---

Weekly Low Stock Alerts cover currently purchasable active materials only.
Zero usable stock is independently eligible even with a blank threshold; this
does not change Stock Status's existing threshold-required stock-alert filter.
Keep the optional threshold and Mix policy for the bound Dilution material.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a separate zero-stock union while
prohibiting changes to unrelated filters, purchasing eligibility or accounting.

**How to apply:** Reuse authoritative usable stock and PO material eligibility;
do not include combos or reserve-only stock or invent replenishment decisions.

Weekly email is Monday at 10:00 AM Asia/Kolkata to the protected active Super
Admin's saved email and one optional shared Settings email, in private separate
deliveries with equal-address deduplication. Consuming a week cannot be undone
by configuration changes, and accepted/uncertain handoffs never auto-resend.

**Why:** The user fixed the schedule, recipient scope and no-backlog policy.

**How to apply:** Preserve recipient-scoped durable outcomes and current-week
claims. This delivery was explicitly source-only, not permission or evidence
for future runtime activation, checks, migrations or live email sends.
